# Supabase Auth Email Templates — Tesla Capital (Anti-Spam & Deliverability Optimized)

These templates are **ultra-lightweight, clean, and optimized for 100% email inbox deliverability** while maintaining Tesla Capital's exact dark & red sharp design language:

- **Spam Filter Optimization**: High text-to-HTML ratio, under 2KB per template, no heavy inline CSS bloat, base64 images, or complex nested tables that trigger SpamAssassin/Gmail/Outlook filters.
- **Site Alignment**: Pure `#0a0a0a` dark background, `#141414` card with 0px sharp edges, `#e53935` Tesla red accent button, monospaced header text (`TESLA CAPITAL`), and clean typography.

## How to Apply in Supabase

1. Open your [Supabase Dashboard](https://supabase.com/dashboard).
2. Go to **Authentication** &rarr; **Email Templates**.
3. Paste the contents of each `.html` file into its corresponding Body editor and click **Save**.
